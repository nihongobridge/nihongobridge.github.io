01 The hidden cost

Japanese players are looking. Your page isn't talking to them.

Japanese players are some of the most willing to pay for games and apps. They strongly prefer games in their own language, and they can spot a machine translation in a single sentence.

01 · SKIPPED

English-only pages get scrolled past

A store page with no Japanese tells a Japanese player, in one glance, that this game is not for them. Many simply move on.

02 · DISTRUSTED

Machine Japanese reads like a warning

Stiff, translated phrasing tells a Japanese player the game was not made with them in mind, and they move on.

03 · REVIEWED

Bad translation stays in your reviews

Steam shows review scores by language. Complaints about Japanese text sit on your page long after launch.

04 · MISSED

A real share of players, left on the table

Publisher Hooded Horse has reported Japanese players at over 6% of the audience on some titles, well above the platform average.

08 FAQ

The questions you're probably asking.

What's the catch with the free store page?

There isn't one. The free page is how developers get to know our work. Some come back for their full game. Many don't, and that's fine. You keep the page either way, and we won't chase you.

Is this just machine translation?

No. AI writes a first draft only. A native speaker then reads and rewrites every line, including tone, character voice and wordplay. You will never receive raw machine output.

What if I don't like the result?

Tell us what feels off and we'll revise it. Two rounds of revisions are included. And since the store page and sample are free, you can judge the quality before paying anything.

What file formats do you accept?

Anything: CSV, JSON, XLSX, PO, Unity or Unreal exports, Google Sheets, or plain text. You get the same format back, with the structure untouched.

How fast is it?

Store pages in 2–3 days. Full localization is typically 1–2 weeks for 10,000 words. We confirm the date with your quote.

How do I pay?

PayPal invoice, 50% to start and 50% on delivery. Larger projects can be split into milestones. You can also order through Fiverr.
