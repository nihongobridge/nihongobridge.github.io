Why Japanese

A small market on paper. A big one in practice.

Japanese players buy less often than you'd think — and spend more than almost anyone when they do. What decides whether they buy is the text.

Top 3Japan is one of the three largest App Store markets in the world by revenue.
Highest-spendingJapanese Steam players are among the highest-spending players per capita.
Native or nothingJapanese players strongly prefer native-quality Japanese. Translated-sounding text is a red flag that hurts wishlists, reviews and sales.
Store page firstA Japanese store page alone usually lifts wishlists from Japan noticeably — before a single line of the game is localized.

What we localize

Everything a Japanese player reads before and after they buy.

Steam store pages

Restructured the way Japanese store pages flow — a one-line summary first, feature sections, the wishlist call-to-action at the end — not just translated.

In-game text

Dialogue, items, UI, tutorials, achievements. Humor and puns are rewritten so they land in Japanese instead of dying in translation.

App Store and Google Play listings

Name, subtitle, description and a keyword set, written within Apple's character limits and without wasting keywords on words already in your title.

A small flat fee per update, so your Japanese stays current every time you ship.

Not a game or an app? We also help with Kickstarter pages, software and SaaS interfaces, websites, board game rules and documentation. If Japanese readers will see it, ask us.

About

Who we are

Nihongo Bridge is a localization studio based in Japan. Our Japanese is written by native speakers, not translated by software and lightly checked, for independent developers who want Japanese players to feel that the game was made for them.

We keep the studio deliberately small. Every line that goes out is read, and usually rewritten, by a native speaker. That is why the Japanese reads like it was written in Japanese, and why you get answers from a person, usually the same day.

How we work

We use AI tools for a first draft, then every line is reviewed and rewritten by a native speaker. That is how we keep prices low without the translated-sounding Japanese that machine translation alone produces.

We say this openly because developers deserve to know how their text is made.

What you can expect

A reply from a person within 48 hours. A fixed quote and delivery date before any work starts. Files back in the format you sent. And if something reads wrong after release, we fix it at no charge.

FAQ

Questions developers ask us

What file formats do you accept?

Anything: CSV, JSON, XLSX, PO, Unity or Unreal exports, Google Sheets, or plain text. You get the same format back, with the structure untouched.

How fast is it?

Store pages within 2–3 days. Full localization is typically 1–2 weeks for 10,000 words. We confirm the delivery date with your quote.

Do you use AI?

Yes, for the first draft only. Every line is then reviewed and rewritten by a native speaker before it reaches you. You will never receive raw machine output.

How do I pay?

PayPal. 50% to start, 50% on delivery. Larger projects can be split into milestones.

Can you help after launch?

Yes. Patch notes, new content, and reading your Japanese reviews so you know what players are saying.

Is the free store page really free?

Yes. No credit, no obligation, no follow-up pressure. We'd rather you see the quality than take our word for it.
